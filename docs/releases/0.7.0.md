0.7.0 brings the lobby's `wip` line into the main release. The builders have been given a map,
the army has been asked to bring everyone home, and several departments have declined to comment.

# What changed since 0.6.0

## Economy

- **Expansion has a destination.** Constructors spread out to take useful ground instead of
  all discovering the same mex. They keep working along the way, and are less easily talked into
  abandoning a nearly finished job for an errand across the map.
- **Power before paperwork.** Energy shortages get builders' attention sooner. Allies budget
  reactors and geothermal projects together, and repairing a broken grid gets a proper crew.
  Owning plenty of power on the wrong side of a disconnected pylon is less reassuring now.
- **Projects get finished.** Builders stick with the work they have been assigned, including
  allied frames. Plates no longer convince the AI it has already bought a whole new factory,
  and a failed factory plan gets another attempt.
- **Less self-imposed siege.** Factories leave room for their units to leave. Armies caught behind
  our own buildings have ways of asking for an exit. Wind farms have been reminded that roads
  are also a renewable resource.
- **Defenses mind their neighbours.** Turrets spread their kinds and their explosions, and more
  thought goes into what they are protecting. A mex that has met a raider remembers the visit.

## Army

- **Better judgement about fights.** The combat forecasts account for more of what happens
  between the first shot and the wreckage: reloads, movement, slowing and capture included.
  Units still make mistakes. They now have more detailed reasons for them.
- **Home defense keeps its distance.** A force sent to screen a raid is less inclined to stand
  inside the raiders' guns while waiting for the right moment. Scouts borrowed for the response
  get their old jobs back afterwards.
- **Heavies and their company.** Packs pay more attention to firing range, escorts and the way
  home. Crabs think harder before climbing down into trouble. Several other units have picked
  up habits best encountered in person.
- **Sieges get support.** Artillery is better screened, busy fronts can call for more of it,
  and the builders are less likely to leave a large investment half built. The planning
  department has also revisited some questions about reach. It won't elaborate.
- **Some early games may feel different.** Please direct complaints to the opposing commander.

## Air and team games

- **Aircraft settle down.** Idle planes find their way home and stay available for another
  sortie, instead of repeatedly rediscovering that they are already home.
- **Air has to earn its keep.** When aircraft stop paying for themselves, production can pause
  and the investment go elsewhere. An air-only ally has alternatives too.
- **Scouting with a purpose.** Reconnaissance is better coordinated, including when the next
  decision needs a look beyond the front. The findings are confidential until further notice.
- **Allied openings leave each other room.** Starts spread out, and planes and gunships share
  the alliance's opening air slot rather than each claiming to be the only air player.

## Defaults

This release keeps the WIP defaults. In particular:

- **Standing ground is back on** (`stayveto`). Hurt units may stay when the forecast says their
  contribution is worth the risk. `disable=stayveto` sends them back to the ordinary retreat rule.
- **The new expansion planner is on** (`unifiedconquest`), as is the finer division of large
  terrain regions (`regionsplit`). `disable=lanes,unifiedconquest` restores the older worker policy.
- Experimental `fightlicense`, `raidcommit`, `harasswide` and `ammo` remain off. Chat commands
  are still off; the banter department remains entirely too available.

## Install

Unzip `ZKGBAI-0.7.0.zip` into the engine's data directory (the one holding `games/` and `maps/`,
e.g. `~/.spring`). It holds `AI/Skirmish/ZKGBAI/0.7.0/` with the Linux and Windows libraries,
and lobbies then offer ZKGBAI 0.7.0.

The libraries were built by this repository's CI; check one with
`gh attestation verify libSkirmishAI.so -R Anarchid/zkgbai-rs`.
